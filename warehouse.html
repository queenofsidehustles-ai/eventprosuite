🏷️ Managing Party Planner Tiers
To change a party planner's library tier, run this SQL in your Supabase dashboard (supabase.com → your project → SQL Editor):

UPDATE profiles SET library_tier = 'founding' WHERE email = 'their@email.com'; Tier values:
• founding — Unlimited slots, gets all future templates for free
• tier1 — Up to 15 templates (default for all new users)
• tier2 — Up to 30 templates
• unlimited — Unlimited slots
