How to Use Party Biz Hub

Everything you need to run your party business — explained simply. No tech experience needed. If you can send a text, you can use this.

👤My Profile 🌐My Website 📅Bookings 💰Quote Builder 📋Contracts 📊Profit Calc 🤝Vendors 📱Content Studio 🔁The Full Flow
👤

Step 1: Set Up Your Profile

Tell the system who you are. This powers everything else.

1
Click "Business Profile" in the left menu
This is where you enter your business name, phone, email, city, and logo.
2
Fill in every field you can
Your business name, your city, your contact info. The more you fill in, the better your website and bookings will look.
3
Upload your logo (optional)
Even a simple logo makes everything look more professional. It shows up on your website and quotes.
4
Click Save
Done. Your profile is set.
💡Do this first. Your city goes on your website and in Google search results. Without it, people searching "kids party in Orlando" won't find you.
🌐

Step 2: Build Your Website

A real website for your business — done in about 15 minutes.

1
Click "My Website" in the left menu
You'll see a 4-step wizard. It walks you through everything.
2
Step 1 — Pick your style
You have 5 design styles to choose from: Luxe (dark & gold), Pop (bright & fun), Studio (clean & professional), Garden (warm & earthy), and Bloom (bold & colorful). Click 👁 Preview this style → to see what each one looks like before you choose.
3
Also in Step 1 — Pick your niche
Choose what type of party entertainment you offer (Balloon Twisting, Face Painting, Princess Characters, etc.). This fills in some starter content for you.
4
Step 2 — Your brand
Enter your business name, tagline, phone, email, and city. Pick your colors. Upload your logo. This is what your clients will see.
5
Step 3 — Your content
Add your packages (names, prices, what's included), your reviews, your story (About section), and photos. The more you fill in, the better the site looks.
6
Step 4 — Preview & download
Click 🔄 Preview to see your live website. When it looks good, click ⬇ Download My Site to get a ZIP file you can upload to Netlify for free.
💡Publish for free in 60 seconds: Go to netlify.com/drop, drag the ZIP file onto the page, and your website is live. That's it.
⭐SEO tip: Make sure your city is filled in under Your Brand. This helps Google show your website when someone searches "kids party in [your city]."
📅

Step 3: Bookings Come In Automatically

When someone fills out your booking form, it lands here instantly.

1
Every new booking shows up on your Dashboard
You don't have to do anything. When a client fills out the form on your website, it appears here automatically with their name, event date, and all the details they gave you.
2
Click any booking to update its status
You'll see 5 options: Inquiry → Quote Sent → Deposit Paid → Confirmed → Complete
3
Mark it Confirmed when they pay
When you mark a booking as Confirmed, the system automatically creates a profit record with the revenue. You'll see a message: "Add expenses →" — click that when you're ready to track your costs.
💡Also shows up here: Quotes you send to clients AND messages from the "Have a question?" form on your website. Everything in one place.
💰

Step 4: Send Quotes to Clients

Build a professional price quote in minutes and email it directly to your client.

1
Click "Quote Builder" in the left menu
This is your pricing tool. You pick items from your catalog, and it calculates everything.
2
Fill in the client's name, email, and event details
The client's email is important — you'll need it to send the quote directly from here.
3
Add your services from the catalog
Click any item on the left to add it to the quote. The total updates automatically.
4
Click "💾 Save Quote"
This saves the quote to your dashboard and makes it shareable.
5
Click "📧 Email to Client"
Your email app opens with a pre-written message — the client's name, event details, total price, and a link to their quote page. Just hit Send.
💡What the client sees: A beautiful page showing everything — their event, every line item, the total, and a "Book My Party" button. Very professional.
📋

Step 5: Send Contracts for Signature

Protect yourself legally. Clients sign on their phone — no printing needed.

1
Click "Contract" in the left menu
You'll see a 6-step wizard that builds the contract for you.
2
Fill in the event details and client info
Business info, client name, email, phone, event date, and service.
3
Pick your contract clauses
Check which rules apply: deposit policy, cancellation terms, weather clause, photo permission, etc.
4
Sign it yourself first
Draw, type, or upload your signature on the page.
5
Copy the client link and send it
A secure link is generated. Text or email it to your client. They open it on any device and sign with their finger. Done — contract locked.
💡Always use a contract. It protects you if a client cancels last minute, if something goes wrong, or if there's a dispute about what was agreed.
📊

Step 6: Track Your Money

Know exactly what you're making (and keeping) after every event.

1
Profit records are created automatically
When you confirm a booking on the dashboard, the revenue is logged for you. You don't have to do anything.
2
Click "Profit Calc" to add your expenses
The revenue is already there. You just need to add what you spent: supplies, travel, labor, etc. The system calculates your profit and margin automatically.
3
Check your monthly report
See total income, total expenses, and profit for the whole month. This is the number you give your accountant at tax time.
💡Keep it simple: After every event, spend 3 minutes adding your receipts. At year end, you'll have everything organized and ready for taxes.
🤝

Step 7: Build Your Vendor Network

Keep your favorite suppliers and helpers organized in one place.

1
Click "Vendors" in the left menu
This is your personal vendor address book.
2
Add every person or company you work with
Balloon suppliers, balloon artists you subcontract, photo booth rentals, cake vendors, venue contacts — anyone involved in your events.
3
Add their category, phone, email, and notes
Notes are great for things like "brings own table" or "needs 45 min setup" or "minimum booking $200."
💡Why this matters: When you're planning an event, you need to reach the right vendor fast. Having them all in one place saves you from scrolling through texts and contacts.
📱

Step 8: Post to Social Media (Content Studio)

Type one idea. AI writes the full caption, hook, story version, and hashtags — ready to post in seconds.

1
Click "Content Studio" in the left menu
You'll see three tabs: Create, My Queue, and Batch Week.
2
Type your hook or idea in the box
It can be anything — "princess party tips for moms," "behind the scenes at a spa party," or "why parents love our packages." You can also tap the 🎤 mic button and speak your idea instead of typing.
3
Pick your Post Type and Platform
Choose what kind of post this is (After Party Recap, Tips & Advice, Package Promo, etc.) and which platform (Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook). Each platform gets a different style of caption automatically.
4
Click "Generate My Content"
In about 5 seconds you'll get: a scroll-stopping hook, a full caption, a short story version, and 30 hashtags — all written for your niche.
5
Attach a photo or video (optional)
Use the "📸 Attach Photo or Video" box on the right to upload your own image or video. It will travel with the post when you publish.
6
Click "Save to Queue"
Your post is saved as a Draft. Switch to the My Queue tab to see all your saved posts.
7
In My Queue — mark posts Ready when you want to publish
Click the 📝 Draft badge on any card to cycle the status: Draft → ✅ Ready → 🚀 Posted. When a post is Ready, it shows up in the purple "Publish All Ready" banner at the top.
8
Click "📤 Publish All Ready" or "Post Now" on a single card
"Publish All Ready" posts every Ready item at once to your connected social accounts. "Post Now" posts just that one card. You need a Zernio API key in your Business Profile to enable publishing.
💡Batch Week tab: Click ⚡ Batch Week to generate 7 post ideas for the entire week in one shot — Monday through Sunday, all different types. Save the ones you like and expand them into full posts.
⭐Consistency is the secret: Post 3–5 times per week. Use the queue to plan ahead. Even 2 posts a week keeps your business visible and brings in more inquiries over time.
🔁

The Full Business Flow

This is how a typical booking works from start to finish.

Follow these steps every time you get a new client:

1 Client visits your website → 2 They fill out the booking form → 3 It shows up on your Dashboard → 4 You send a Quote → 5 You send a Contract → 6 Mark as Confirmed (revenue logged) → 7 Do the event → 8 Mark as Complete → 9 Add expenses in Profit Calc

That's it. That's your whole business system.

⭐Time estimate: Once you're set up, handling a new booking from first contact to confirmed takes about 10–15 minutes. Most of it is automated.
