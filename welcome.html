Step 1 — Your Store Name & Link

Your store lives at partybizhub.com/store/YOUR-NAME — this is the link you share everywhere

1
Go to your Digital Store
Click Party Profit Printables in the left sidebar.
2
Find "Your store address" at the top
You'll see a field next to partybizhub.com/store/
3
Type your handle
Pick something short and easy — like princess-party-co or mias-printables. Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. No spaces.
4
Click Save Handle
Your store link is now live. Copy it and save it!
Tip: Keep it short. People will type this in their phone and share it with friends. Something like partybizhub.com/store/star-parties is perfect.

Step 2 — Connect Your Payment

Pick ONE option — this is how money comes to you when customers buy

You only need one. Pick the easiest one for you right now — you can change it later.

PayPal — Easiest. Most people already have it.
  1. Go to paypal.com/paypalme and sign in (create a free account if needed)
  2. Choose a username for your PayPal.me link
  3. Copy your link (looks like paypal.me/YourName)
  4. In your store: scroll to "About You & Contact" → paste it in Payment Link → Save
Stripe — Most professional. Customers pay by card (no PayPal needed).
  1. Go to dashboard.stripe.com — create a free account (need a bank account to receive money)
  2. Click Payment Links on the left → click Create
  3. Name it anything and set a price → copy the link (starts with buy.stripe.com)
  4. Paste it in your store's Payment Link field → Save
Square — Good if you also sell in person at events.
  1. Go to squareup.com → create a free account
  2. Go to Online Checkout → Create a payment link
  3. Copy the link → paste it in your store's Payment Link field → Save
Do this before sharing your store: Click your own payment link and make sure it works. Test it yourself first.

Step 5 — How to Edit Templates in Canva

Canva is free. If you can type a text message, you can do this.

When your customer downloads their purchase, they get a file with Canva links inside. Here is how it works:

1
Open the downloaded file
The file your customer downloads has Canva links listed inside — one for each item in the bundle.
2
Click any Canva link
It opens in the browser. Canva asks you to make a copy. Click Use Template. A free Canva account is all you need.
3
Click on any text to change it
Just click the text (like the guest's name or party date) and type the new text. It is exactly like editing a text message.
4
Change colors if you want
Click any colored shape → click the color box at the top → pick a new color. You can match the party theme perfectly.
5
Download when done
Click Share (top right corner) → Download → choose PDF Print for best quality. Ready to print!
Money tip: Many sellers charge an extra $10-20 to customize the template for the customer (add the name, change colors). This is called a "done-for-you" add-on. Customers love it and it is easy extra income.

Printing Guide

Where to print and how to handle physical orders if your customer wants them

Where to get items printed:
Staples or Office Depot — Great quality. Upload your PDF online or go in person. Same-day pickup available. Good for cardstock.
Walmart Photo Center — Affordable and fast. Upload online and pick up in about an hour.
Canva Print — Order directly from Canva when you download. Ships to your door in a few days.
Home Printer — Works great. Regular paper for chip bags and labels. Cardstock for toppers.
Paper to use:
Chip bags, water bottle labels, Capri Sun labels → Regular copy paper
Cupcake toppers, favor bag toppers, treat boxes → Cardstock (holds up better)
Sticker designs → Sticker paper (find on Amazon or Walmart)
Premium finish → Glossy photo paper
How to seal chip bags:
1
Print your chip bag design and cut it out
2
Open an individual snack-size bag of chips (1 oz size works best)
3
Wrap the printed design around the bag like a sleeve
4
With heat: Use a hair straightener on LOWEST heat. Slide it along the top and bottom of the bag. Hold 2 seconds. Let cool.
5
No heat option: Use double-sided tape on the inside edges of the paper to hold it around the bag.
Pro tip: Buy chip bags in bulk at Costco or Sam's Club. Individual snack-size bags fit most templates perfectly.

Custom Domain (Optional)

You do not need this — your partybizhub.com link works perfectly. But here is how if you want one.

Remember: Your partybizhub.com/store/your-handle link is free and works great. A custom domain is a nice extra — not a requirement.
Where to buy a domain (about $10-15 per year):
Namecheap (namecheap.com) — Cheapest option, very beginner-friendly
Google Domains (domains.google.com) — Easy if you already use Google
GoDaddy (godaddy.com) — Popular, good customer support
Squarespace Domains — Good if you already use Squarespace
How to forward your domain to your store:

This makes yourdomain.com redirect to your partybizhub.com store. Like a postal forwarding address for your website.

Namecheap:
Log in → Manage your domain → Redirect Domain → paste your partybizhub.com/store/your-handle link → Save.
GoDaddy:
Log in → My Products → your domain → Manage DNS → Forwarding → Add → Domain → paste your store link → Save.
Google Domains:
domains.google → your domain → DNS → Synthetic records → Subdomain forward → paste your store link → Save.
Note: After setup, it takes 24-48 hours to activate. That is normal.

Your Questions — Running This Business

Plain answers, no tech speak

Do I need to know anything about design? ▾
No! The designs are done for you. You only change text (like a name or date) in Canva. If you can type a text message, you can do this.
What price should I charge my customers? ▾
Most digital party bundles sell for $8-18. Start at $9.99 to get your first sales. After you have reviews and traffic, raise your prices. Check Etsy for "party printables" to see what others charge.
Can I change colors to match any party theme? ▾
Yes! Colors, text, and names are all editable in Canva. You can turn any design into any color your customer wants. Characters and images in the design are fixed, but everything else is flexible.
What if my customer does not have Canva? ▾
Canva is free and most people can learn it in 10 minutes. You can also offer to customize the template FOR them and charge extra — many customers love the "done for you" option. Charge $10-20 more for this service.
Do I need to print or ship anything? ▾
No shipping ever! You sell a digital file. Your customer downloads it, edits it in Canva, and prints it themselves. You never touch a printer. That is the beauty of this business model.
Can I sell products that are not in the library? ▾
Yes! Use the "Add Product" button in your store to add any digital product you want. You are not limited to the library. Your own designs, your own files — anything goes.
What is my refund policy? ▾
Most digital sellers have a no-refund policy since the file cannot be returned. Add a note to your store: "Due to the digital nature of this product, all sales are final." But always offer to fix problems or exchange for another design — that keeps customers happy.
How does my customer get their file after they pay? ▾
After they pay through your Stripe or PayPal link, they get redirected to your store with a download button. They click it and get the file instantly. They can also type their email on that page to get a copy sent to their inbox.
I need help — who do I contact? ▾
Email support@partybizhub.com and we will get back to you within 24 hours.
