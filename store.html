🚀 Getting Started

Follow these 5 steps to get your store live and ready to sell.

1
Complete your store profile

Go to the My Store tab → scroll down to "About You & Contact." Add your business name, a short bio, your logo, and contact email. This shows on your public store and helps customers trust you.

2
Set up your payment link

You need a way to collect money. Pick one option and paste the link in the Payment Link field in your store profile:

PayPal (easiest — most people already have it)
Go to paypal.com/paypalme → create your PayPal.me link → paste it in the Payment Link field. Free to set up.
Stripe (most professional)
Go to dashboard.stripe.com → Payment Links → Create. Set a product price → copy the link → paste it here. Requires a bank account to activate.
Square (good if you also do in-person sales)
Go to squareup.com → Online Checkout → Create a payment link. Copy and paste it here.
3
Add your first product
  1. 1Click Add Product button at the top of the My Store tab
  2. 2Click ✨ Quick Fill — type a theme name (e.g. Unicorn) → click Auto-fill ✨
  3. 3This fills in the full bundle name and SEO description — all 11 items listed automatically
  4. 4Set a price — your store payment link (set in Step 2) handles checkout automatically
  5. 5Upload a photo of your printable bundle — customers buy what they can see
  6. 6Click Save Product — it appears on your store instantly
4
Set your store handle and share your link

In the My Store tab, find the Your store address on partybizhub.com field. Type a short handle (example: bear-hugs-events). Your store link becomes partybizhub.com/store/your-handle. Copy it and share it everywhere — Instagram bio, Facebook, Linktree, text messages.

5
Pick your store theme

Back in the My Store tab, scroll up to the Store Template section. Pick the look that matches your brand — Studio (clean, light), Luxe (dark, gold), Bloom (soft pink), or Pop (bold). Your store updates instantly.

❓ Common Questions
Do my customers download instantly?
Yes — once they pay through your payment link, you send them the Canva template link or file. For fully automated delivery, use Gumroad as your payment processor (it handles delivery automatically).
Can I sell products I created myself — not just the Party Profit Printables templates?
Yes — you can add any digital product to your store. The Quick Fill is pre-loaded with the Party Profit Printables templates, but you can type any product name and description manually.
How do I customize the names (like changing "Unicorn" to "Dinosaur")?
You customize in Canva. Open the Canva template link, click the text you want to change, type the new name. Download as PDF Print. Done. Your graphic designer included step-by-step Canva instructions with your template package.
What if I need help?
Email support@partybizhub.com and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
